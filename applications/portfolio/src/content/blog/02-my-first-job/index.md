These are my raw feelings at 2 am on a saturday.

About 2 months ago, I quit my first full time software engineering job. It was a great experience to say the least. But what did I quit for? another corporate literally 2 levels down.

Why? I don't know...

The people were great, the projects were great but the pay was pretty bad but they countered (just a bit lower) and I didnt stay. I told myself, its a change...change is good. Different yet similar work that I get to try and challenge myself with yet my life remains the same.

I see my old manager as I'm leaving work one day...he asks having regret?

I joke but really i do regret it. I was doing well there, work was comfortable and i was enjoying it. But leadership scares a restructure...and I jump ship as soon as I can.

The survival instinct...It's important and look my new job isn't bad or anything. The people are great, the projects are actually great too and the pay is great. Nothing is wrong. Yet...

I feel like I made a mistake. and im not sure why...

I know theres a lot of people who say work is work and it doesn't matter but its literally 40 hours of my life a week. It's a massive commitment of my life. It's not to be taken lightly.

Funnily enough, I join the new company and a few days later, at a townhall they announce they will not be doing outsourcing / layoffs and the feeling of being a winner was quite calming.

But then I realised...

My "40 hours" a week got largely disrupted when I didn't want it to be. 

At this stage, I have felt like the decisions of others, namely large corporations shouldn't make me feel this way and I'm writing this because I've decided that I don't want this feeling again.

I don't want my identity tied to work. I don't want my feeling of daily life tied to work that I cannot control.

Do I want to find a start up for my self? maybe
Do I just want to open a cafe cuz I love coffee? most definetly
Will these happen soon? probably not
But I hope that I'm able to grow and feel more sure of myself again.
